===== [p326-326] 20.1.1 Exposure Processing and System Parameters (1块) =====
--[p326]-- System parameters are used for the system operations.

System parameters basically consist of parameters that define the flow and conditions of processes related to exposure.
These specifications are referenced during exposure processing.
